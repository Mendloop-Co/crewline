**This is the index for working on this repository from another team or Claude account. Don't discuss here: open one issue per topic.**

**You are not one of the home team's agents.** Organise and name your own agents however suits your team. Start with "Who you are" in the rulebook, then "Collaborating from another team or account" in the contributing guide.

## Joining
1. Pick a short team name: one lowercase word, not in the table below.
2. Open `[notice] New team: <team>`, labelled `collaboration` and `for:<home_team>`.
3. Start your team's watcher (required): `/loop /crewline:watcher <team>`.
