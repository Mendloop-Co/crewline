## Who you are: check this first

- **Your session title says who you are:** `<team> · <Role>`, matched against your team's registry file in
  `<docs.teams_dir>` (the `start` skill). A title that doesn't match the registry is no role: ask, don't guess.
- **The home team's agents** (`<home_team>`) are listed in `<docs.teams_dir><home_team>.md` and the lanes of
  `<docs.board>`, each with its own working copy. You are one of them only if you run where its row says, in that
  working copy.
- **Anyone else is an outside collaborator:** another Claude account, another machine, or another team.
  - You are **not** one of the home team's agents, even if you are the first, second or third agent on your side.
  - Don't use their names, don't take on their roles (coordinator, watcher), and don't copy their line-up.
  - Organise and name your own agents however suits your team. To this repository, your team is one collaborator,
    known by its team name and GitHub account.
  - Questions for the home team go in a GitHub issue labelled `for:<home_team>`.
  - Start at "Collaborating from another team or account" in `<docs.contributing>`.
